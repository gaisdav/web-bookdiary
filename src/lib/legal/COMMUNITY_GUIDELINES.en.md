# Readimus Community Guidelines

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Last updated: 5 October 2026

Readimus is a space for readers to keep a personal library, publish reviews,
discuss books, and share their reading activity. We want it to be an
interesting, welcoming, and safe place to connect. These Guidelines explain
what helps create that environment and what can undermine it.

These Guidelines apply to all content that users make available to others,
including profiles, avatars, lists, reviews, comments, book information, and
reading activity.

When creating an account, you must accept our [Terms of Use](/terms) and these
Guidelines. By publishing content or interacting with other users, you agree
to follow them.

## 1. Treat others with respect

The following are not allowed:

- bullying, harassment, intimidation, or humiliation of others;
- threats of violence or wishes of harm;
- hateful or discriminatory content based on race, nationality, ethnic
  origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or
  any other protected characteristic;
- sharing another person's personal or confidential information without
  permission;
- impersonating another person or organization in order to mislead others.

You are welcome to disagree with a book, an author, or another reader's
opinion—lively discussion is part of the experience. Please challenge ideas,
not people: threats, insults, and targeted harassment have no place on
Readimus.

## 2. Do not publish dangerous or illegal content

Content is not allowed if it:

- violates applicable law;
- promotes violence, terrorism, criminal activity, or harm;
- contains material depicting sexual violence or exploitation;
- sexualizes minors or puts their safety at risk;
- contains pornographic imagery or sexually explicit material published
  primarily for sexual gratification;
- encourages self-harm or dangerous behavior;
- is used for fraud, phishing, or distributing malware.

You may discuss books intended for mature audiences and the difficult topics
they address, as long as the discussion relates to the book and does not turn
into the publication of material prohibited above.

## 3. Respect creators' rights

You may add books and edit book information in Readimus. Titles, author names,
ISBNs, and other bibliographic details may be entered manually. Creating a
book listing does not mean uploading the book itself: Readimus is not intended
for publishing the full text of books.

Readimus obtains some book information and cover images from external book
databases. If you add or replace a description, cover image, other image, or
any other material yourself, you are responsible for that material and must
have the rights or another lawful basis to use it. Do not upload the full text
of a book, substantial excerpts, or other protected material without
permission or another lawful basis.

Quotations and discussions of works are permitted to the extent allowed by
applicable law. Credit the source where appropriate.

## 4. Promote books appropriately and do not spam

Authors, publishers, and other members of the book community may talk about
their books, events, and projects when relevant to the discussion. Please be
transparent about your connection to what you are promoting, and do not post
the same message repeatedly, in bulk, or intrusively.

The following are not allowed:

- repeatedly posting identical or irrelevant content in bulk;
- undisclosed, misleading, or non-book-related advertising;
- artificially inflating likes, follows, ratings, or other activity;
- creating accounts or publishing content through automation without
  permission from Readimus;
- deceptive links or attempts to redirect users to unsafe resources.

## 5. Write honest reviews

Reviews must reflect your genuine opinion. You may not buy, sell, or commission
reviews that are required to be positive or negative, or offer compensation
in exchange for a particular rating.

You may receive a free review copy from an author or publisher. If you publish
a review afterward, clearly disclose the free copy or any other relationship
you have with the author, publisher, or book. Receiving a free copy does not
oblige you to give the book a high rating or write a positive review.

## 6. Provide accurate book information

Users may add and edit information about books. Do not knowingly submit false
information or replace book information with advertising, jokes, or unrelated
content.

You can report an incorrect title, author, description, or cover using the
“Report an issue” option on the book's page.

## 7. Mark spoilers

You may discuss a book's plot and ending, but please use the available spoiler
label. Do not post significant plot details where other users would not
reasonably expect to see them.

## 8. Report violations

If you find a violation of these Guidelines, use the “Report” option next to
the relevant content or profile. You can also send us a link and a brief
description of the issue at `support@readimus.com`.

We review reports within a reasonable timeframe based on the nature and
severity of the violation. Reports involving an immediate safety risk, child
exploitation, or other potentially dangerous content are prioritized. Complex
cases may take longer to review.

You can block a user if you do not want to see their content or interact with
them.

Do not include unnecessary personal information in your report. If there is
an immediate threat to someone's life or safety, contact your local emergency
services first.

Reports of potential copyright infringement are handled under our separate
[Copyright / Takedown Policy](/copyright).

## 9. Moderation and enforcement

Our goal is not to punish people for honest mistakes, but to maintain a safe
and welcoming community. We consider the context, severity, and frequency of
a violation. Depending on the situation, Readimus may:

- warn the user;
- limit the visibility of or remove content;
- temporarily restrict certain features;
- temporarily suspend or permanently terminate an account;
- retain information needed to investigate a violation or comply with legal
  obligations, in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and applicable law;
- contact the relevant authorities when required by law or necessary to
  protect people's safety.

These measures do not have to be applied in order. We will generally start
with the least severe appropriate measure, but a serious safety threat or
severe violation may result in the immediate removal of content or termination
of an account.

You may not create or use other accounts to evade a block, account suspension,
or another moderation action. Those accounts may also be restricted or
terminated.

## 10. Ask us to review a decision

We can make mistakes too. If you believe an action was incorrectly taken
against your content or account, contact us at `support@readimus.com`. Include your
username, the decision you would like us to review, and why you believe it was
incorrect.

## 11. Changes to these Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines as Readimus evolves, new features are added, or
legal requirements change. The current version and the date it was last
updated will be published on the Readimus website.

Material changes will take effect on the date stated in the updated version.
