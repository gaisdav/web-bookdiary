# Readimus Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Last updated: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Readimus collects and uses, why
we process it, who may receive it, and what rights users have.

This Policy applies to the Readimus application, website, and other services
(together, “Readimus” or the “Service”).

## 1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The controller of your personal data is Gaisar Davletkildin, an individual
operating Readimus from Serbia (the “Operator,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

For questions about personal data or to exercise your rights, email
`privacy@readimus.com`.

## 2. Data We Process

Depending on how you use Readimus, we may process the following categories of
data.

### 2.1. Account and Profile Data

- email address;
- your password in a protected form, as processed by the authentication system;
- full or display name;
- username;
- profile description and avatar;
- internal user identifiers;
- privacy and community settings; and
- dates relating to account creation and changes, email confirmation, and
  account sign-in.

We do not receive your password in plain text and cannot view it.

### 2.2. Guest Access Data

When you use guest mode, Readimus creates a technical anonymous account and
associated internal identifiers and session data. That account does not contain
your real email address, name, or username unless you choose to convert it into
a standard account.

### 2.3. Book and Reading Data

- books in your library and lists;
- reading status and reading start and finish dates;
- ratings, likes, and the history of changes to your reading activity;
- lists you create, including their titles, descriptions, and visibility
  settings;
- book, author, cover, and edition information that you add or suggest; and
- search queries, ISBNs, and book identifiers from external sources.

### 2.4. User and Social Content

- reviews, comments, and spoiler labels;
- book notes and images attached to them;
- reading activity posts and reactions to them;
- follows, followers, and follow requests;
- reports, selected report reasons, and information submitted with reports; and
- information about moderation measures and requests to review decisions.

### 2.5. Communications With Us

When you contact us, we may process your email address, name, the content of your
message, attachments, and information needed to respond or investigate an
issue.

### 2.6. Technical Data

When the Service operates, we and our infrastructure providers may
automatically process:

- IP address;
- information about your browser, application, device, and operating system;
- the date, time, and outcome of a network request;
- account and session identifiers and authentication tokens;
- technical logs, error information, and security events; and
- your selected language and limited network information needed for the
  application to function.

As of the date of the latest update, Readimus does not use separate SDKs for
advertising tracking, product analytics, crash reporting, or push notifications.

## 3. Device Permissions and Local Data

If you choose to upload an avatar, a note image, or a book cover, the application
may request access to your camera or photo library. Access is used only after
your action and only to the extent necessary for the feature you selected. You
can revoke permission in your device settings.

Readimus stores session data, cached books and search results, a profile
snapshot, and interface settings on your device. This helps keep you signed in
and makes the application work faster. Local data may remain until you sign out,
clear the application’s data, or uninstall the application, unless a shorter
retention period applies to a particular record.

## 4. Where We Obtain Data

We obtain data:

- directly from you when you register, complete your profile, publish content,
  or contact support;
- automatically when you use the application or website;
- from your interactions with other users;
- from Google Books, ISBNDB, and other lawful sources of book information; and
- from app stores and infrastructure providers where necessary to operate,
  secure, or support the Service.

## 5. How We Use Data

We use personal data to:

- create and maintain accounts, confirm email addresses, and restore access;
- provide guest mode and convert a guest account into a standard account;
- maintain your library, reading diary, lists, notes, and other selected
  features;
- display your profile and content in accordance with your privacy settings;
- provide follows, comments, reactions, in-app notifications, and other
  community features;
- search for books and maintain the quality of the book catalog;
- receive and review reports, moderate content, and review decisions;
- respond to user communications;
- protect users and the Service and prevent spam, fraud, unauthorized access,
  and circumvention of restrictions;
- diagnose technical problems and maintain the reliability of the Service; and
- comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.

We do not use personal data for third-party advertising and do not sell it.
Readimus has no paid features, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or payment data
processing. We use email only for service messages related to your account,
security, support, and operation of the Service, not for promotional mailings.

## 6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires us to identify a legal basis, we rely on one or
more of the following:

- **performance of a contract** — to create your account and provide features
  you request under the [Terms of Use](/terms);
- **legitimate interests** — to maintain security, prevent abuse, moderate the
  community, improve the reliability of the Service, and protect our legal
  rights, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and
  freedoms;
- **legal obligation** — where data must be processed or retained to comply with
  the law or a lawful request from a competent authority; and
- **consent** — where consent is required for a particular optional activity.
  You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing that
  took place before withdrawal.

Account data identified as required is necessary to create a standard account.
Without it, the relevant features may be unavailable.

## 7. Data Visibility and Public Content

Your username, display name, avatar, profile description, reviews, comments,
public lists, social connections, and reading activity may be visible to other
users depending on the type of content, your profile settings, and the community
features you enable.

Your email address, password, access tokens, private notes, and note images are
not intended for public display.

Do not add information to your profile, review, comment, or other public
material that you do not want to disclose. Other people may copy or retain
content that has already been published independently of Readimus.

## 8. Book Search and External Sources

When you search external book databases, your search query, an ISBN, or a book
identifier may be sent through Readimus infrastructure to Google Books or
ISBNDB. Readimus does not intend to send your name or email address to these
services as part of such a request.

Search results and search query keys may be temporarily cached on the server to
speed up search and keep it working when an external source is temporarily
unavailable. Search results are normally current for 7 days and may be retained
for up to another 30 days as a fallback if a source fails. Information about an
individual book is normally current for 30 days and may be retained for up to
another 30 days. An expired cache entry may be removed the next time the cache
is updated. This cache is shared among users and is intentionally not associated
with a particular account.

## 9. Who We Share Data With

We share data only to the extent necessary for the purposes described in this
Policy with the following categories of recipients:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, file storage, and server-side
  functions;
- **Google Books and ISBNDB** — book search and retrieval of book metadata and
  covers;
- **Vercel** — temporary hosting for the website and account-related pages;
  this Policy will be updated if the hosting provider changes;
- **email delivery and communications providers** — delivery of service
  messages and handling of communications;
- professional advisers and contractors who need access to provide services and
  are required to maintain confidentiality;
- public authorities, courts, and other persons where disclosure is required by
  law or necessary to protect rights, safety, the Service, or its users; and
- a successor to the Operator if Readimus is transferred to a company or
  another owner in accordance with applicable law.

We do not share personal data with data brokers and do not sell it.

Apple and Google may independently receive technical data and app store account
information when you download or update the application. Their own policies
govern that processing. Readimus does not ordinarily provide them with your
profile data or User Content for application distribution.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Readimus is available to users worldwide and is operated from Serbia. Our
service providers and their subprocessors may process data in other countries.
The primary region of the Supabase project is Central Europe (Frankfurt,
Germany).

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international data transfers, we
use legally recognized mechanisms, such as an adequacy decision, standard
contractual clauses, or other permitted safeguards.

## 11. How Long We Retain Data

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, taking
security and legal requirements into account.

- standard account data and associated content are retained for as long as the
  account exists, unless a shorter or longer period is stated below;
- abandoned guest accounts are automatically deleted after 30 days of
  inactivity;
- data on your device is retained as described in Section 3;
- the server-side book search cache is retained as described in Section 8;
- support communications are normally retained for up to 2 years after the
  request is closed;
- reports and moderation records are normally retained for up to 3 years after
  the report is closed;
- technical logs are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to
  maintain security, investigate incidents, and diagnose technical problems;
  and
- data remaining in backups is deleted through the normal backup rotation
  process within 7 days.

Some data may be retained for longer where necessary to comply with a legal
obligation, maintain security, prevent repeated violations, resolve a dispute,
or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. At the end of the applicable
period, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 12. Account Deletion

You may delete a standard account through the application settings. When you do
so, Readimus deletes the account, associated profile, library, lists, reviews,
comments, social connections, notes, avatar, and note images, except for data
that must be temporarily retained for the reasons described in Section 11.

Book information and covers added to the shared catalog may continue to exist as
part of the shared book database if they are no longer associated with you and
do not identify you.

Files in backups may remain for a limited time until their scheduled deletion.
If an individual file is not removed automatically because of a technical
error, we may remove it after we discover the issue or receive a request from a
user.

You can also request account and associated data deletion through our external
[Account deletion](/delete-account) page.

## 13. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- learn whether we process your personal data and obtain access to it;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request deletion of data;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive data you provided in a portable format;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, email `privacy@readimus.com`. We may request
information necessary to verify your identity and protect your account. We will
respond within the period required by applicable law.

You may contact the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance
and Personal Data Protection or, where applicable, the data protection authority
in the country where you live or work.

## 14. Children’s Data

Readimus is intended for people aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children under 16.

If a user has not reached the age of majority in their country, they may also
need permission from a parent or legal guardian in accordance with the Terms of
Use and applicable law.

If you believe that a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, email
`privacy@readimus.com` so that we can review the situation and take appropriate
action.

## 15. Automated Decision-Making

As of the date of the latest update, Readimus does not make decisions based
solely on automated processing that produce legal effects for a user or
similarly significantly affect them.

## 16. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data,
including access controls, database-level permission separation, secure data
transmission, and authorization checks for account deletion.

No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure. If you believe
your account or data has been compromised, email `support@readimus.com`.

We may update this Policy when Readimus, our data processing practices, or the
law changes. The current version and the date of the latest update will be
published on the Readimus website.

We will notify you of material changes through the application, by email, or by
another appropriate method where required by law. If consent is required for new

## 18. Contact Us

For questions about this Policy or personal data, email
